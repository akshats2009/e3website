Grants for Veteran-Owned Businesses

This list gathers 6 grants for veteran and military-spouse entrepreneurs, plus nonprofits that serve veterans and their families. Awards range from $1,000 micro-grants to housing grants of up to $500,000 for nonprofits. 1 is open now, 2 accept applications on a rolling basis, and 3 are closed for now. Many of the closed programs run every year, so they are worth tracking.

List last updated November 13, 2026. Search all 87 grants in the Grant Finder.

Before you apply

  • Check the funder's page. Deadlines, award amounts, and rules change often. Treat this list as a starting point.
  • Read the eligibility details closely. Ownership share, years in business, revenue range, and location differ a lot between grants.
  • Plan for the next cycle. Many closed programs recur annually, so note their usual timing and prepare early.
