Grants for Women-Owned Businesses

This list gathers 8 grants for women entrepreneurs and women-led organizations, from monthly micro-grants to awards of up to $100,000. Most are for small businesses; one supports nonprofit domestic violence shelters. 3 accept applications on a rolling basis, and 5 are closed for now. Many of the closed programs run every year, so they are worth tracking.

List last updated November 13, 2026. Search all 87 grants in the Grant Finder.

8 women-owned business grants

WomensNet

Amber Grant for Women

RollingSmall businessWomen-Owned

Monthly $10,000 grants for women-owned small businesses across multiple categories (Amber, Startup, and Business Category), with year-end winners receiving an additional $50,000. A $15 one-time application fee considers applicants for all monthly categories.

Award$10,000 monthly, plus $50,000 year-end
DeadlineRolling / ongoing

Before you apply

  • Check the funder's page. Deadlines, award amounts, and rules change often. Treat this list as a starting point.
  • Read the eligibility details closely. Ownership share, years in business, revenue range, and location differ a lot between grants.
  • Plan for the next cycle. Many closed programs recur annually, so note their usual timing and prepare early.
