Before you apply
Check the funder's page.

Deadlines, award amounts, and rules change often. Treat this list as a starting point.

Read the eligibility details closely.

Ownership share, years in business, revenue range, and location differ a lot between grants.

Plan for the next cycle.

Many closed programs recur annually, so note their usual timing and prepare early.
