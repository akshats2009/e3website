Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The e3 Initiative is a small research organization. This page explains what information this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Contact form

If you send us a message, we receive the name, email address, subject and message you enter. We use them only to reply to you. The form is delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party service, which processes your submission in order to email it to us.

Analytics

We use two analytics tools to understand which pages are read and how people find us:

  • Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies and records page views, approximate location, device and browser information. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool we host ourselves. It records page views and basic device information without tracking you across other websites.

We do not use analytics data to identify individual visitors, and we do not sell it.

Settings saved in your browser

When you change the theme or text size, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it persists between visits. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, requested page and time) for security and operations.

Third-party content

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash. Your browser contacts those services directly when it loads those files, so they may receive your IP address and browser details.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works normally either way.
  • You can ask us to delete any message you sent through the contact form.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date above.

Questions

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please use our contact form.
