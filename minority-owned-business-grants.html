Grants for Minority-Owned Businesses

This list gathers 12 grants for founders and organizations from communities that have historically had less access to capital, including Black, Native American, Hispanic/Latino, and LGBTQ+ business owners. The funders range from national foundations and corporations to a state commerce department. 2 are open now, 3 accept applications on a rolling basis, and 7 are closed for now. Many of the closed programs run every year, so they are worth tracking.

List last updated November 13, 2026. Search all 87 grants in the Grant Finder.

12 minority-owned business grants

Black Ambition (founded by Pharrell Williams)

Black Ambition Prize

OpenSmall businessMinority-Owned

A national competition funding early-stage businesses with at least one Black, Hispanic/Latino, LGBTQ, first-generation immigrant, or other underrepresented founder, in consumer products, healthcare, media, technology, or AI. Ventures must have raised less than $1 million in dilutive funding.

AwardUp to $1,000,000 in prize funding across awardees
DeadlineRecurs annually
View & apply →

Montana Department of Commerce

Tribal Tourism Small Business Grant

OpenSmall businessMinority-Owned

Grants for start-up or expanding Native American-owned tourism businesses in Montana. Funded from Montana's Lodging Facility Use Tax, with a total of $240,000 for FY2026.

AwardUp to $10,000 per business (no match required)
DeadlineNov 30, 2026
View & apply →

Founders First CDC

Founders First Job Creators Quest Grant

RollingSmall businessMinority-Owned

Grants for diverse-led service businesses or light manufacturers with 2 to 20 employees and $100,000 to $3 million in revenue that can add premium-wage jobs. Diverse founders include Black, Indigenous, people of color, LGBTQIA+, veterans, women, or those in low-to-moderate-income areas.

AwardCash award plus full-tuition accelerator scholarship
DeadlineRolling / ongoing
View & apply →

RollingSmall businessMinority-Owned

Cash grants for Black women and Black nonbinary entrepreneurs with a legally registered, scalable business who plan to seek investor financing. Awardees also receive fundraising guidance and lifetime access to the SoGal Foundation and SoGal Ventures teams.

Award$5,000 and $10,000 cash grants

Before you apply

  • Check the funder's page. Deadlines, award amounts, and rules change often. Treat this list as a starting point.
  • Read the eligibility details closely. Ownership share, years in business, revenue range, and location differ a lot between grants.
  • Plan for the next cycle. Many closed programs recur annually, so note their usual timing and prepare early.
